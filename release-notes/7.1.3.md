## Fixed

- After a lost connection to an MQTT broker that wants a login, the bridge logs in again. Since 7.1.0 every
  automatic reconnect was sent without username, password and last will: a broker without anonymous access refused
  it (`not authorised` in the broker log) on every attempt, for as long as the bridge ran, and only a restart of the
  bridge brought it back. That happened after every restart or replacement of such a broker. The last will is back
  on every reconnect too, so a bridge that disappears after a reconnect is marked `offline` again.
