## Fixed

- After a reconnect to the MQTT broker, a subscribe that got no answer could block every incoming command for good,
  while the bridge kept reporting itself online. Calls to the broker now give up after 10 seconds, the resubscribe no
  longer runs on the thread that executes commands, and a failed resubscribe is tried again every 5 seconds (at most
  5 times) as long as the connection stays up.
- Commands that arrive while the central unit is unreachable wait for it at most 30 seconds from their arrival, and
  are then dropped with a warning (`MQTT topic '...' not executed: waited 30 seconds for the central unit`). Before,
  commands queued during a long outage could still be executed minutes later, once the line came back.
- A corrupt message from the central unit no longer costs the messages around it: the bridge resynchronises on the
  next message start instead of dropping everything it received. A wrong checksum is now logged as a warning
  (`Receive - Skipping bytes with a wrong checksum`) instead of an error.
